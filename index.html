Done privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Done blocks the apps you choose while you're at places you choose. It's built to keep your information on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your locations (names, map positions and sizes), app lists, schedules and settings.
  • How long you've been blocked, and how much time you spend at each of your locations (the last 35 days).
  • Your app choices are stored as private codes from Apple; Done can't see which apps they are.
  • Screen Time information, such as your most used apps and your weekly screen time, is shown inside Apple's Screen Time system and never leaves your iPhone or reaches Done.

What leaves your iPhone

  • When you save a location, Done sends that location's map area once to an OpenStreetMap data server (overpass-api.de) to draw its small map preview. The preview is then kept on your iPhone. Like any website, the server can see your device's internet address when it answers.
  • Anonymous usage counts, such as how many people finish setup or turn on emergency access, sent to TelemetryDeck to help improve Done. They contain no Screen Time data, no locations and nothing that identifies you.
  • Done has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking.

Location

Done uses your location, including in the background ("Always"), only to tell whether you're at one of your saved places. Your movements aren't recorded or sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

In Done, go to Settings > Delete data to erase everything Done has stored. Deleting the app also removes it.
